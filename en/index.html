    Still unsure?

    Fair enough. These are the questions we get most often.

    Isn't a website too expensive for my business?

    A €900 site you use for 3 years costs about €25 a month. One extra customer or member a month pays for it. The price is fixed, so you know upfront what you pay.

    I have Facebook and Instagram. Isn't that enough?

    Your social media isn't yours: the algorithm decides who sees your posts. Your website is your own place. We link your social media to it, so every post sends people to one place.

    Isn't my Google profile enough?

    Your profile and your site work together. People choose first on clear, complete information, then on reviews and clear prices. That doesn't all fit in a profile. We set up your profile too.

    Do you use automations? Isn't that impersonal?

    Our digital assistants handle the repetitive work: numbers, updates, checks. Alec built and trained them himself. He designs your site and checks everything, and a group of real people tests every site before it goes live. You always have Alec on the line.

    What if Dobbo ever stops?

    Your site and domain are in your name and everything is documented. If we ever stop, everything stays yours and someone else can take over.

    How do I know if it pays off?

    You see it yourself: a dashboard with your visitors and how often people tap call or directions, plus a report from the owl.

    Do I have to take the monthly care plan?

    No. You can cancel monthly and your site stays yours, even without a care plan.

    I don't have a site yet. Is that okay?

    Yes, then we start from scratch.
